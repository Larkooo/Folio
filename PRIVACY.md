# Folio Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Folio is a book reader for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## What Folio collects

Folio does not collect any information about you. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not connect to any server run by its developer or by anyone else.

## Your books and reading data

The books you add, their covers, your reading positions and your settings are stored only on your device, inside Folio's own storage. They are not sent anywhere. If your device backs up to iCloud or to a computer, that data is included in the backup in the same way as other app data, under Apple's terms.

When you add a book from the Files app or open one from another app, Folio makes a copy of that file in its own storage. Removing a book from your library deletes that copy and your place in it.

## Children

Folio does not collect information from anyone, including children.
